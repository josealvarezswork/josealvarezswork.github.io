two of product systems. That framing holds whether the next role is product or
creative, so none of these has to be rewritten when that decision lands.

| Length | Opens on | Because |
|---|---|---|
| headline | what he builds | nobody reads past the first clause in a feed |
| one line | the reader's benefit | it has to work with no context around it |

| 90 | the framework | ESA is the thing nobody else has |
| 150 | the four years | at this length the span is the strongest fact |

matters to a hiring manager is not the subscriber count — it's **"a system
absorbed four years of change without being rebuilt."** That sentence is about
maintenance, which is what systems work actually is.

### Qué cambia respecto al inglés

Tres cosas, y ninguna es de traducción:

- **"Bucles de progresión"**, no "loops". *Loop* suelto en español lee a
  anglicismo perezoso; *bucle* es la palabra y se entiende igual en el gremio.
- **"A cargo del sistema visual"** en vez de *owning*. No hay equivalente
  limpio de *own* en este sentido, y "ser dueño de" suena raro. "A cargo" dice
  responsabilidad sin sonar traducido.
- **"34.500"** con punto, no coma. Es la convención en Colombia, y una coma ahí
  lee como decimal.
