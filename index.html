Cuak Fintech · Mobile
✦ What works

The emotional hook is real. 5/5 prototype testers felt more in control immediately after seeing their credit usage with mascot feedback. Externalizing financial anxiety into a reactive character is validated behavioral design — the concept lands every time.

◆ The blocker

Open Banking in Latin America isn't ready for solo builders yet. Belvo confirmed no credit card data coverage in Colombia. Pivoting to Mexico — where the API exists — means rebuilding market context, legal structure, and user trust from scratch. Hard to do alone.

DuoGit Mobile App
✦ What works

Developer activity data is cold and judgmental by default. DuoGit reframes it — same data, different emotional register. The behavioral design system makes progress visible without making absence shameful. The concept is solid and the emotional reframing works.

◆ The blocker

The target user — a developer motivated enough by their GitHub activity to pay for an emotional overlay — is a narrow niche. Distribution without an existing developer audience is expensive. The product works. Getting it in front of the right people without paid acquisition is the unsolved problem.
